Forge Doorbell

Forge Doorbell is a private, personal-use tool run by Andras Hangyal. It sends short notification emails from his own Gmail account to his own Gmail addresses, so his personal automation knows when new work is waiting.

It uses one Google permission, send email on your behalf (gmail.send). It does not read, search or delete mail, and it is not offered to the public.
